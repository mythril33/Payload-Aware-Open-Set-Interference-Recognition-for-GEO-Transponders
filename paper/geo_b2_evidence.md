## Numeric display audit

The validator checks Table5's exact rounded physical values against committed JSON pointers, verifies both reused figure identities and the unchanged old manuscript source, and independently reconstructs role/sample/batch arithmetic. It also checks citation/evidence coverage, all declared future display statuses and minimum manuscript structure. It does not verify a reference model against hardware, recompute diagnostics, run bootstrap samples, simulate a scene, fit a network or open final observations.

## Interpretation and version resolution

P18–P22 contain historical estimates, pending decisions and development defaults. The manuscript resolves training/loss/numerics against the later E9 ratification and exposure/statistical amendments against E12. It does not amend those records. P20 canonical `B2-feat/1` and P21b `B2-features/1` remain distinct. The15-fit campaign excludes optional co controls; recorded21-fit all-tier budgets are not presented as a requirement. Mahalanobis remains a recommendation, not approved primary. Existing physical figures are not promoted to H1–H3 recognition evidence.
