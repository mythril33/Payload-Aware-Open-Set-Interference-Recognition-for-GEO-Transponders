## Scientific guardrails for prose

1. Novelty is a specific controlled conjunction of observation point, payload traceability, dual monitoring, plan-derived residual and matched OSR contrasts. Do not claim first satellite ML, first cancellation detection, first open-set RF recognition, or systematic proof of first GEO OSR. The independent review contains strong negative literature claims and industry percentages that are not suitable for transfer into journal prose.
2. Henarejos and Pellaco use actual received/captured signals. Correct distinction: digitally mixed interferers or spectrum anomaly prediction do not establish an uplink-interference traversal through a controlled nonlinear IMUX/TWTA/OMUX model. Do not say all prior satellite learning work is simulated.
3. ETSI reference models are engineering traceability, not empirical calibration of this simulator to a particular spacecraft; the project does not implement complete DVB-S2 framing/coding.
4. The two-channel monitoring contract is an explicit availability/receiver calibration assumption, not a claim that all GEO operators have calibrated dual-polarization monitoring. The co-only canceller must not consume cross IQ or simulator truth.
5. Local center loss is batch mean of summed squared embedding distances, coefficient0.005; Wen original uses one-half times a sum and separate center learning-rate update. Cite inspiration and state the implemented convention explicitly.
6. Local Mahalanobis covariance is `(1-a)S + a*(tr(S)/D)I + floor*I`, `a=0.1`, `floor=max(1e-6*tr(S)/D,1e-8)`, and residual Gram denominator max(N-K,1). It shrinks to trace-scaled identity, not diagonal, and uses Cholesky/triangular solves. Fit train only, at the val-select-selected checkpoint. Tiny one-group training makes residual covariance zero and is numerically extreme; pilot score behavior is not recognition evidence.
7. Energy scoring should not be described as energy-based model training. Higher scores mean more unknown-like across all scorers. Mahalanobis remains recommended, not selected/approved primary.
8. Frozen higher empirical val-calib quantiles at nominal0.95 known acceptance and clean0.05 Pfa are design operating points, not population guarantees or a conformal coverage theorem; correlated snapshots/variants reduce effective independent calibration units.
9. H1 adds the cross monitor in the plan-conditioned regime; H2 adds a plan-dependent co-only observed residual to already dual observations; H3 compares matched planmask/noplan controls with plan-independent level normalization and no plan-derived auxiliary target or plan-shift augmentation in either arm. H2 cannot establish plan-independent cancellation benefit.
10. Existing engineering and physical diagnostics support execution/observability checks, not full-scale recognition superiority, causal mechanism identifiability, measured satellite performance or sim-to-real validation. All unexecuted H1–H3 recognition measurements must remain NOT RUN, including null cells rather than zero metrics.
